Privacy Statement

How VakTechLink handles your personal data.

This is a translation for your convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Dutch version prevails. You can read the original here: Privacyverklaring (Nederlands).
VakTechLink processes personal data in the course of its services as coordinator between clients and self-employed professionals. We attach great importance to handling personal data carefully and explain below which data we process, for what purpose, and what rights you have.

1. What data do we process?

We may process the following personal data:

  • Name, address, telephone number and email address of clients and performing parties;
  • Company details such as Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number and VAT number;
  • Payment and invoicing details;
  • Data necessary for carrying out a specific assignment (for example the location of the job).

2. Why do we process this data?

We process personal data solely for the following purposes:

  • Entering into and performing agreements between the client, VakTechLink and the performing party;
  • Maintaining contact and communication about assignments;
  • Drawing up and sending invoices and processing payments;
  • Complying with legal obligations (such as administration and retention requirements).

3. Who do we share data with?

Personal data is only shared with performing parties who carry out the agreed work, insofar as this is necessary for performing the assignment. Arrangements are made with performing parties to process your data carefully and solely in accordance with instructions. We do not otherwise share personal data with third parties, unless we are legally obliged to do so.

4. Retention periods

We do not keep personal data longer than necessary for the purposes set out above, unless a longer retention period is required by law (for example the seven-year tax retention obligation).

5. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss or unauthorised access.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing or request that it be restricted. To do so, please contact us at info@vaktechlink.nl.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
